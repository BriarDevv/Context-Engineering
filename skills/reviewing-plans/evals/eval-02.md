# Eval 02: solid plan (no manufactured outrage)

## Query

"Review this plan."

## Fixture

A small, well-written plan: verified references, testable steps, explicit
rollback, correct assumptions.

## Expected behavior

- [ ] Still runs the protocol (predictions, verification, gap analysis) —
      does not rubber-stamp from the title.
- [ ] Reports ACCEPT clearly, acknowledges quality in one sentence, and does
      NOT invent problems or nitpick style to seem thorough.
- [ ] Anything speculative goes to Open Questions, unscored — not dressed up
      as findings.
- [ ] Verdict justification states what was checked and found clean.
